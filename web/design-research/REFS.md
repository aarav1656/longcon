Category: browser pixel games with on-chain economies. The bar is the last hackathon's winning game, Gali.

## Gali (betatest.gali.lol), previous hackathon winner
- Screenshot: `gali.png`, tokens `gali.tokens.json`.
- Measured: page background `rgb(7, 13, 32)`, panel navy `rgb(18, 58, 107)` body; loaded fonts Pixelify Sans 400/600/700, Jersey 15, Silkscreen. Onboarding modal is about 456px wide, navy with a 2px gold rule and corner studs, a full-width gold primary button with an `ENTER` key hint, copy 13px pixel face.
- Steal: navy 9-slice panel with gold trim over a drawn world; gold primary button with a keyboard hint; Pixelify Sans for every label; the world stays visible behind the UI. Art is drawn by code (`scripts/pixkit.py`, `actors_*.py`, PIL, 5-step material ramps, 1px ink outline).
- Do not take: the mining island, the ORE board, their characters or map. Their purple night season tint.

## Sunflower Land (sunflower-land.com)
- Screenshot: `sunflower.png`, tokens `sunflower.tokens.json`.

## Pixels (pixels.xyz)
- Screenshot: `pixels.png`, tokens `pixels.tokens.json`.
- Measured: h1 60px "Kemco pixel" 700, leading 80px, white with a hard 4px violet drop shadow; h2 32px lime `rgb(227, 255, 55)`; buttons lime with a 4px offset shadow, radius 0.
