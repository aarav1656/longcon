# DESIGN.md: Long Con (market road)

Replaces the Hallmark "Grid" ledger theme (2026-11-13). Research: `design-research/REFS.md` (Gali, Sunflower Land, Pixels, Danger is NEAR; negative: the old ledger page).

## Thesis
A pixel market road at golden hour. You stand at a merchant's stall and talk them out of their gold; the contract's verdict lands as a wax seal on the counter.

## Primary job of the screen
Pick a town, pitch its merchant, see the seal (advanced, refused, timed out) and what the road allowed. Everything else sits below the stage.

## Art
- Generated by code: `web/art/*.py` (PIL) into `web/public/art/`. Native 320x180 scenes, scaled by whole pixels, `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- 5-step material ramps (wood, cobble, cloth, skin, gold), light from the upper left, 1px ink outline `#1c161e`, dithering only at sky band edges.
- Three towns: mill (red awning, windmill), gate (teal awning, gatehouse), port (blue awning, harbour). Three merchants with 7 frames each: idle0, idle1, blink, talk0, talk1, pleased, stern.

## Type
Pixelify Sans 400/600/700 (self-hosted, `@fontsource/pixelify-sans`) for everything players read. Hashes and accounts: system monospace 12px. Scale 12 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 26 / 30 / 64. Tabular figures on numbers. Headings roman, never italic.

## Shape
Radius 0. Panels are a 24px 9-slice (`art/panel.png`, slice 8, drawn at 16px): navy fill, gold bevel, corner studs. Buttons are 16px 9-slices with a pressed state (gold primary, green confirm, navy secondary). Depth is a hard ink edge and a 6px offset drop, no blur.

## Layout
HUD chips (day, gold, bond, credit, account) -> road map strip with three town tokens -> 16:9 stage (town scene, merchant behind the counter, town sign, news board, quest line, seal) -> dialogue (portrait, the merchant's line, pitch choices, wager, Pitch button | verdict) -> purse and debts | con ledger -> road record.

## Motion
Merchant idle and blink by `steps()`; talk loop after a new verdict, then pleased (advanced) or stern (refused). Seal slams in (scale and rotate, 5 steps); coins spin to the purse on an advance. Buttons lift 2px on hover, drop 2px on press. All motion off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Bans
Purple fields, beige or parchment backgrounds, blurred glows, decorative particles, italic headings, emoji icons, em dashes in copy.
